\begin{center}
{\bfseries\large How the Implementation Reflects and Differs from the Proposal}
\end{center}

\vspace{6pt}

The project was implemented substantially as proposed: a Naive Bayes baseline and Bayesian
Network structure learning with Hill Climbing, the three-way stats / market / combined ablation,
Gaussian Naive Bayes for the continuous features, and the proposed dataset of 3{,}990 NFL games
over 15 seasons joining nflverse statistics to Sportsbook Review market data.

Three elements differ from the proposal:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{PC algorithm --- expanded.} Proposed as a secondary validation check, it became a
  central result: run independently, it confirmed the corrected direction of a causally impossible
  edge that Hill Climbing had produced, turning the dual-algorithm comparison into a contribution
  rather than a footnote.

  \item \textbf{Result framing --- refined.} The proposal anticipated a binary outcome --- either the
  network beats the baseline, or the closing line is efficient. The results supported a more specific
  conclusion: market signals do not improve accuracy but do improve calibration, i.e.\ partial
  efficiency.

  \item \textbf{Metrics --- reduced.} The proposal listed Brier score, with AUC-ROC as optional. The
  final evaluation reports accuracy and log loss.
\end{itemize}

All other elements were delivered as proposed.
